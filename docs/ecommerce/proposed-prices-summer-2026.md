## Local delivery

### Delivery policies

- Local delivery available to a limited area (see [Zip codes](#zip-codes))
    - CT deliveries are limited to meat only
    - RI deliveries can include meat and/or eggs
- Deliveries are made weekly on Thursday
- Orders must be pre-paid
- Coolers must be left outside and accessible for drop off
- Not available for orders under $50

### Delivery fees

- Delivery fee: $10
    - Delivery fee is waived on orders greater than $150

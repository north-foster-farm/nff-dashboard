- need to clarify the purpose of the DNF and what I mean by accountability. Chores are a team effort—the goal is not a 50/50 split where I do half and my dad does half. Whoever is available does the chores. There is no need to consider per-participant completion percentage—this implies a system in which the person who completes more chores is doing more than their share and the other person is slacking. This just isn't the reality—our effort is distributed across a lot more than just chores, so this dichotomy isn't real, and the completion comparison between participants isn't telemetry I want to surface.
- I should have thought a bit more carefully about introducing the concept of DNF. Chores all must be finished, period—this is a farm, so not doing chores means animals don't eat or drink. The accountability I'm seeking has more to do with improving our consistency and time management. One big issue we are trying to solve right now is how to prevent our chores from getting strung through the whole day: chores were started late, then someone has to jump to something else before chores are done, then we go back to chores, then project timelines are getting derailed because it's already lunch and we're still feeding animals, then pretty soon it's already time to check the brooder but we haven't made enough progress on projects yet, and so on. I believe the primary reason for this lack of consistency and organization comes down to a failure to build and maintain effective routines around doing chores—they should happen at the same time every day and take about the same amount of time. Accountability in this case means we know when chores were started and finished within the designated block of time. When either of these two conditions are not met, these are the failure states we care about and this is the telemetry we need. But even if our chores run over time by 20 or 30 minutes, they still must get done—"not finishing" is not an option. The interaction regarding DNF still sounds like it makes sense, but needs to be revised based on this clarification.
